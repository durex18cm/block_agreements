🎮玩法介绍

  • 棋盘为 8×8，底部每次出现 3 个方块。
  • 拖动方块放入棋盘空格，3 个方块全部放置后会自动补充新方块。
  • 当某一整行或整列被填满时，该行或列会立即消除并腾出空间。
  • 当当前方块都无处可放时，本局游戏结束。
  • 设置中可切换经典、小姐姐、小哥哥、萌宠、赞赞虎、雨燕、自定义等方块样式。
小技巧：优先保留大块空间，尽量为长条和大面积方块预留落点，连续消除能获得更高连击分。

🏆计分与奖励

  • 放置方块：每个小格 +1 分。
  • 消除行列：每消除一行或一列 +10 分。
  • 连击：连续多次放置都触发消除时，连击倍数会递增。
  • 同时消除：一次放置同时消除 n 行/列时，消除得分翻 n 倍（消 2 行 ×2、消 3 行 ×3，以此类推）。
  • 完美清屏：清空整个棋盘可获得额外 +100 分。

登录后，游戏成绩可用于排行榜、金币奖励和相关活动结算。具体奖励比例、榜单周期、奖励内容和领取条件以应用内展示为准。

🪙金币与复活

每局结束后，系统可能根据本局得分、累计局数和应用内配置发放待解锁金币。金币相关能力属于应用内在线功能，具体规则以应用内页面和应用内规则为准。

游戏结束后可能提供复活机会，常见方式包括：

  • 免费复活：每日可能提供一定次数。
  • 广告复活：观看广告后继续游戏，开放状态和次数以应用内展示为准。
  • 金币复活：消耗金币后继续当前局面。

复活后分数保留，连击状态可能重置。

❓常见问题

游戏数据丢失了怎么办？

本地设置和自定义图片保存在设备本地，更换设备或重新安装应用后可能无法自动恢复。登录后的排行榜成绩、金币和部分服务器数据以服务器记录为准。如发现异常，请通过邮件联系我们。

如何关闭音效或震动？

进入游戏后，点击设置图标，可以分别开关音效和触感反馈（震动）。

如何使用自定义方块图片？

进入设置，选择方块样式中的"自定义"，再打开自定义图片管理，为不同颜色组选择并裁切正方形图片。应用仅使用您主动选择的图片。

游戏需要网络连接吗？

基础游戏体验可在本地运行；登录、排行榜、金币奖励、复活、活动配置、广告复活、邮件联系等功能需要网络连接。

为什么需要登录？

登录后可以保存战绩、参与排行榜、查看金币余额、领取奖励或使用应用内在线功能。未登录时，部分功能可能不可用。

排行榜分数多久更新？

排行榜通常会在游戏结算后更新，但可能受到网络状态、服务器处理、榜单周期或风控校验影响。如长时间未更新，请通过邮件联系我们。

广告复活无法使用怎么办？

请检查网络连接、当天可用次数和应用内提示。广告复活可能受广告库存、应用内配置或版本状态影响，并非所有时间都可用。

如何反馈 Bug 或提出功能建议？

请通过邮件联系我们，并尽量提供问题描述、复现步骤、截图或录屏、应用版本、设备型号和系统版本。

🎮How to Play

  • The board is 8×8, and three blocks appear at the bottom each turn.
  • Drag blocks onto empty cells. After all three are placed, new blocks appear.
  • A full row or column clears immediately and frees space.
  • The round ends when none of the current blocks can be placed.
  • You can switch block styles in settings, including classic, beauty, guys, pets, Zanzanhu, Swift, and custom styles.
Tip: preserve large open spaces and plan for long or bulky pieces. Consecutive clears can earn higher combo scores.

🏆Scoring and Rewards

  • Placing blocks: +1 point per cell.
  • Clearing rows or columns: +10 points per cleared row or column.
  • Combos: consecutive clearing moves increase the combo multiplier.
  • Simultaneous clears: clearing n rows/columns with a single placement multiplies the clear score by n (2 lines ×2, 3 lines ×3, and so on).
  • Perfect clear: clearing the whole board grants +100 points.

After sign-in, scores may be used for leaderboards, coin rewards, and related event settlement. Reward rates, leaderboard cycles, reward contents, and claiming conditions are subject to in-app notices.

🪙Coins and Revivals

At the end of a round, the system may grant locked coins based on your score, round count, and in-app configuration. Coin features are online app features and are subject to in-app rules.

After game over, revival options may include:

  • Free revival: a limited number may be available each day.
  • Ad-based revival: continue after watching an ad, subject to availability and daily limits.
  • Coin-based revival: spend coins to continue the current round.

After revival, your score is kept and combo state may reset.

❓Frequently Asked Questions

My game data is gone — what happened?

Local settings and custom images are stored on your device and may not restore automatically after reinstalling or switching devices. Signed-in leaderboard, coin, and server data are based on server records. If something looks wrong, please contact us via email.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?

Tap the settings icon in-game to toggle sound effects and haptic feedback on or off independently.

How do I use a custom block image?

Open Settings, choose the Custom block style, then open custom image management. Select and square-crop images for each color group. The app only uses images you actively choose.

Does the game require an internet connection?

Basic gameplay can run locally. Sign-in, leaderboards, coin rewards, revivals, event configuration, ad-based revivals, and email support require network access.

Why do I need to sign in?

Sign-in enables score saving, leaderboards, coin balance, rewards, and other online app features. Some functions may be unavailable without sign-in.

When does the leaderboard update?

Leaderboards usually update after game settlement, but network conditions, server processing, leaderboard cycles, or risk checks may delay updates.

Why is ad-based revival unavailable?

Please check your network, daily limits, and in-app notices. Ad-based revival may depend on ad inventory, in-app configuration, and app version status.

How can I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Please contact us via email and include a description, reproduction steps, screenshots or recordings, app version, device model, and system version where possible.
