This page lets Yah Blocks users request deletion of their app account and associated data. You can also initiate account deletion inside the App.

📱App Covered

  • App Name: Yah Blocks
  • Developer: Miko Studio
  • Platforms: Android / iOS / iPadOS

🧭How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data in either of the following ways:

  • In the App: After signing in to Yah Blocks, use the cancel account/delete account entry in the account, settings, or profile area.
  • Web request: Send an account deletion request to the email below. Please use the email or sign-in method associated with your account where possible, and include “Delete Yah Blocks account” in your message.
💬
Account Deletion Contact fastable#proton.me (please replace # with @ before sending)

To help us identify your account, please include the app name, your account nickname or account ID, sign-in method, device system, and a clear statement that you want to delete your account and associated data. Please do not send passwords or verification codes by email.

🗑️Data We Delete

After verifying your request, we will delete or anonymize personal information and app data associated with your Yah Blocks account that we still store, including:

  • Account identifiers, nickname, avatar, sign-in status, session credentials, and related account information
  • Server-side gameplay scores, high scores, round counts, leaderboard records, and reward claim status
  • Coin balance, locked coins, revival records, event or task status, and other virtual asset data
  • Support communications, handling records, and necessary API logs associated with the account

After account deletion, you will no longer be able to sign in with that account, and related leaderboard, coin, reward, history, and online service data may not be recoverable.

💾Local Data

Data stored locally on your device, such as local high score, sound settings, haptic settings, block style, language preference, and custom image files you selected, is usually stored on your device.

You can delete local data through in-app settings, your system app settings by clearing app data, or by uninstalling the App. We cannot remotely delete files that exist only on your device.

📌Data We May Retain

Where permitted or required by law, we may retain necessary data for security, anti-cheat, dispute resolution, audit, tax, compliance, or to protect users and the service.

  • Security, risk control, anti-cheat, and abnormal behavior logs may be retained for a reasonable period needed to complete security review or risk handling.
  • Support, complaint, dispute, or compliance records may be retained until the matter is resolved and for any period required by applicable law.
  • Aggregated or anonymized data that can no longer identify a specific user may be used for statistics, service improvement, or compliance purposes.

Unless a longer period is required by law, we will delete or anonymize retained data when it is no longer needed.

⏱️Processing Time

After receiving your request, we will usually complete verification and processing within a reasonable time. In general, we aim to complete deletion or respond with status within 30 days. If the request is complex, legally restricted, or requires additional security verification, we will try to inform you of the expected timing.

Account deletion is permanent. Please make sure you have saved any information you want to keep before submitting your request.
