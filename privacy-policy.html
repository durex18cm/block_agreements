Miko Blocks · 呀方块

生效日期：2026 年 6 月 5 日  |  最后更新：2026 年 6 月 5 日

Miko Blocks（呀方块）仅在实现游戏、账号、排行榜、金币奖励、复活、客服与合规所必需的范围内处理信息，并尽力保护您的个人信息安全。

📋概述

本隐私政策说明了 Miko Blocks（呀方块，以下简称"本应用"）在您使用过程中如何收集、使用、存储、共享和保护您的信息。

本应用包含本地游戏体验，也包含登录、排行榜、金币奖励、复活、活动配置、广告复活和邮件联系等联网功能。不同功能处理的信息类型不同，您可以根据需要选择是否登录或使用相关功能。

📥我们可能收集的信息

为提供和维护本应用服务，我们可能在必要范围内处理以下信息：

  • 账号信息：当您登录应用内账号时，可能处理账号标识、昵称、头像、登录状态、会话凭证等信息
  • 游戏数据：分数、最高分、累计局数、消除行列、复活记录、排行榜成绩、奖励领取状态等
  • 虚拟资产数据：金币余额、待解锁金币、奖励配置、复活消耗、活动或任务相关状态
  • 设备与网络信息：设备型号、操作系统、应用版本、渠道、网络状态、请求时间、接口日志、错误信息等
  • 偏好设置：语言、音效、振动、方块样式、自定义图片配置等
  • 联系与反馈信息：您通过邮件提交的问题描述、截图、联系方式或处理记录

💾本地存储的数据

本应用会在您的设备本地存储以下信息，用于保存游戏偏好、提升启动速度和支持离线体验：

  • 最高分：您的历史最高游戏分数
  • 音效设置：音效开关状态
  • 震动设置：触感反馈开关状态
  • 方块样式：您选择的方块外观主题
  • 自定义图片文件：您选择并裁切后的本地图片文件及其路径（如已设置）
  • 语言偏好：您选择的界面显示语言
  • 登录状态缓存：在您登录后，为保持会话和减少重复登录而保存的必要凭证

自定义图片主要保存在您的设备本地应用目录中。与账号、排行榜、金币奖励、复活、活动结算相关的数据可能会上传至服务器，以便实现相应功能。

📷相册权限

当您使用"自定义图片背景"功能时，本应用会请求访问相册或系统图片选择器。此权限仅用于让您选择本地图片并裁切为方块背景。

我们不会主动读取您的完整相册，不会上传您未选择的图片。您可以随时在系统设置中撤销此权限；撤销后自定义图片功能可能不可用，但不影响基础游戏功能。

🌐网络访问

本应用会在您使用登录、用户信息、游戏开始/结算、金币余额、排行榜、排行榜奖励、复活、邀请码、激活码、活动配置、广告复活、统计分析、问题反馈等功能时发起网络请求。

如果网络不可用，上述联网功能可能无法使用；部分本地游戏体验和本地设置仍可在设备上运行。

🤝第三方服务

为实现应用分发、登录、统计分析、广告复活、崩溃排查、安全风控和合规能力，我们可能接入应用商店、广告服务、统计分析服务、云服务或其他必要第三方服务。

第三方服务可能根据其自身隐私规则处理必要信息。我们会基于合法、正当、必要原则进行接入，并要求第三方依法保护您的信息。

👶儿童隐私

我们非常重视未成年人个人信息保护。未成年人使用本应用前，应取得父母或其他监护人的同意，并在监护人指导下使用本应用及相关在线服务。

如监护人认为未成年人向我们提供了个人信息，或希望查询、更正、删除相关信息，请通过邮件联系我们。

🔐信息安全与保存

我们会采取合理的技术和管理措施保护您的信息，防止未经授权的访问、披露、篡改或丢失。由于互联网环境并非绝对安全，我们无法保证信息传输或存储的绝对安全。

我们仅在实现本政策所述目的所必需的期间保存您的信息。超过保存期限后，我们将依法删除或匿名化处理，法律法规另有要求的除外。

🧭您的权利

您可以在系统设置中管理相册、通知、网络等权限，也可以通过邮件联系我们处理隐私相关请求。

Miko Blocks processes information only as reasonably necessary to provide gameplay, accounts, leaderboards, coin rewards, revivals, support, and compliance services, and we work to protect your information.

📋Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Miko Blocks (the "App") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the App.

The App includes local gameplay as well as network features such as sign-in, leaderboards, coin rewards, revivals, event configuration, ad-based revivals, and email support. Different features process different categories of information.

📥Information We May Collect

To provide and maintain the App, we may process the following information as necessary:

  • Account information: account identifier, nickname, avatar, sign-in status, and session credentials when you sign in
  • Gameplay data: score, high score, round count, clears, revival records, leaderboard results, and reward status
  • Virtual asset data: coin balance, locked coins, reward configuration, revival costs, and event or task status
  • Device and network information: device model, operating system, app version, channel, network status, request time, API logs, and error information
  • Preferences: language, sound, haptics, block style, and custom image configuration
  • Support information: issue descriptions, screenshots, contact details, or handling records you submit via email

💾Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device to save preferences, improve startup speed, and support local gameplay:

  • High Score: Your all-time best score
  • Sound Settings: Whether sound effects are enabled
  • Haptic Settings: Whether haptic feedback is enabled
  • Block Style: Your chosen block appearance theme
  • Custom Image Files: selected and cropped local image files and paths, if configured
  • Language Preference: Your selected display language
  • Sign-in Cache: necessary credentials used to maintain your session after sign-in

Custom images are primarily stored in the App's local directory on your device. Account, leaderboard, coin reward, revival, and event-settlement data may be uploaded to our servers to provide those features.

📷Photo Library Permission

When you use the "Custom Image Background" feature, the App may request access to your photo library or system photo picker. This permission is used solely to let you select and crop an image as a block background.

We do not proactively read your full photo library and do not upload photos you did not select. You may revoke this permission in system settings; doing so may disable the custom image feature but will not affect basic gameplay.

🌐Network Access

The App makes network requests when you use sign-in, user profile, game start/end settlement, coin balance, leaderboards, leaderboard rewards, revivals, invite codes, activation codes, event configuration, ad-based revivals, analytics, or support features.

If network access is unavailable, these network features may not work; some local gameplay and local settings may still run on your device.

🤝Third-Party Services

To support app distribution, sign-in, analytics, ad-based revivals, crash diagnostics, security, risk control, and compliance, we may integrate app stores, advertising services, analytics services, cloud services, or other necessary third-party services.

Third-party services may process necessary information under their own privacy rules. We integrate such services based on legality, legitimacy, and necessity, and require them to protect your information in accordance with applicable law.

👶Children's Privacy

We take minors' privacy seriously. Minors should obtain consent from a parent or guardian before using the App and related online services, and should use them under guardian supervision.

If a guardian believes a minor has provided personal information to us, or wishes to access, correct, or delete such information, please contact us via email.

🔐Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Because the internet is not absolutely secure, we cannot guarantee absolute security of transmission or storage.

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy. After the retention period, we will delete or anonymize it unless otherwise required by law.

🧭Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; withdraw authorization; sign out of or cancel account services; and request explanations about this Policy.

You can manage photo library, notification, network, and other permissions in system settings, and you can contact us via email for privacy-related requests.

🔄Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you by updating the "Effective Date" on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed.
